- Version: 1.6.0
- Milestone: M15.1 — Drip, Prérequis & Unlock Rules
- Date: 2026-09-03
- Cumulative: M01 → M15.1

- Packaged dependencies: intentionally excluded (`vendor/`, `node_modules/`)
- Provider/runtime secrets: never packaged
